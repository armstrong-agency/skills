# The brief

Size it to the job: one section with no new pieces needs a few lines. Cover:

- **Destination:** default to a new unpublished sandbox page.
- **Sections, in order:** each one's responsibility, its Figma node or export, its developer notes, and the closest existing component, class or live section. A familiar name is only a candidate, so inspect its actual responsibility, breakpoints and consumers.
- **Assemble or invent:** whether each section ships from existing pieces or needs something new. Before proposing CMS fields, collections or Designer setup, check whether existing data already answers the need, and offer that first.
- **Invent list:** every new class, combo, component and variable, with what was considered, why it fails, the new piece's distinct responsibility, and its proposed name.
- **Frozen:** what must not change.
- **Acceptance checks:** measurable targets at the four breakpoints when a design source exists.
