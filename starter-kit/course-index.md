## Where this kit fits

Lessons 1 to 3 give AI the context about your brand and your customers. Lesson 4 is where it starts changing your site, and the other three files in this kit are for that step:

1. **The scorecard** tells you whether your site is ready.
2. **The MCP cheatsheet** connects your AI tool to one site safely.
3. **The `AGENTS.md` template** gives the AI its rules.
